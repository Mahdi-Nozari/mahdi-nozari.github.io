# Visual academic portfolio concepts

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. These are design mockups for comparison, not implemented website pages. Research imagery is illustrative; implementation should use Mahdi's actual project media. The separate RollyPoly page is outside this redesign scope.

The homepage emphasizes one short introduction, a distinctive visual, and three research previews. Publications, experience, and detailed CV content belong on linked pages.

## H — Research Atlas

Image: [concept-h-research-atlas.png](concept-h-research-atlas.png)

Prompt:

```text
Use case: ui-mockup.
Generate one polished, realistic desktop homepage screenshot for Mahdi Nozari's personal academic portfolio. Approximately 4:3 landscape artboard. Entire top-of-homepage composition visible with header, a compact introduction, a tasteful graphical area and three visual research previews. No device frame, browser chrome, collage of multiple websites or angled mockup.
The previous concepts were rejected as boring, text-heavy online resumes. This revision must have much more visual interest and MUCH LESS text while retaining a credible personal academic character. Calm and simple, intriguing through imagery, artful typography and composition. The first screen should have only a short introduction and three research previews. DO NOT show publication citations, education lists, employment timelines, skill lists, bullet paragraphs, or a CV document on the homepage. Those belong behind navigation links. Do not fill whitespace with extra text.
Exact name: "Mahdi Nozari". This is an individual mechanical engineer/researcher, not a brand selling products. Do not add Dr., Professor or PhD.
Small top navigation: "Research", "Publications", "Experience", "About". A small "CV" text link may appear.
Introductory text, exactly one sentence: "I study robotic and mechatronic systems through mechanical design, control, and experiments."
A quiet short discipline label: "Mechanical engineering · Robotics".
Research section heading: "Selected research".
Three visual preview titles only: "Soft robotics", "Robotic finger", "Medical imaging prototype". Each title has an image and a small subtle arrow link, no descriptive paragraphs. Illustrative images are concept placeholders: a polyhedral frame robot with soft pneumatic actuators, a single-finger mechatronic experiment, and a small ring-shaped imaging gantry prototype. Avoid commercial polished product advertising; show research process through CAD, sketches, experimental setups or considered prototype photography. Do not invent measurement values, graphs with numbers, credentials, citations, award claims, availability or endorsements.
Optional footer links: "Email", "Google Scholar", "GitHub".
Do not generate a fake portrait of Mahdi, a large initials avatar, or a humanoid stock robot. No "hire me", clients, contact-sales buttons, slogan about building products, ecommerce styling or sales funnel.
Keep typography legible and enough empty space that the page is relaxing to scan. No heavy card borders, shadows, gratuitous decorations, neon, cyberpunk or presentation slide aesthetics. RollyPoly's separate page is outside scope.
Visual direction: An airy visual research atlas in white, deep ink-blue type and restrained muted teal and copper accents. A large beautifully typeset "Mahdi Nozari" headline sits beside a generous editorial scientific illustration: delicate fine-line mechanical diagrams and one small translucent polyhedral robot rendering arranged as a coherent research sketch, with only two tiny labels "Geometry" and "Control", lots of white space and a few fine connectors. Make the graphical composition clearly organized, not a technical dashboard or a wall of equations. Beneath, three generous clean image-first research previews, approximately equal width, with a mix of real-looking laboratory photography and pale CAD drawings, each title below in understated typography. Restrained elegant sans-serif with small technical caption style. Strong graphical personality but a calm academic homepage. Only the name, one-sentence intro and required small labels: no additional paragraphs.
```
